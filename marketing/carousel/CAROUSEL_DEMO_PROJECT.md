# Carousel demo project: *Equal Temperament*

A marketing-only fictional novel, written for the Instagram carousel so it shows Sutura holding a book that is *not* the film's. It lives only in `marketing/src/creatives/carousel/content.ts` and is never written to any database.

- Not the founder's manuscript.
- Nothing from *The Salt House*.

**Genre:** contemporary literary fiction, family drama.
**Writer (pen name on the account):** Miriam Holt.

## Premise

Inês Carvalho, a piano tuner who has lived in Lyon for twenty years, comes home to Porto when her father has a stroke. Rui is a retired orchestral violist, and the stroke leaves him with aphasia: he can no longer say most words, but he can still hear. Over one winter she tunes the old upright in his flat on Rua das Flores. Slowly she understands that the single note he has kept flat for forty years is the closest thing to a sentence he has left.

**Why it suits the carousel:**
- It is grounded and quiet, and different in kind from the film's mystery.
- Its research is a craft (pitch, temperament, beats), so the Canvas has a real *thought* to follow rather than a clue to solve.
- Its payoff is a writing decision, not a reveal: she doesn't correct the note; she tunes the piano to him. That makes *Follow the thought → get back to the sentence* honest.

## Research (a folder of Pages)

- **Pitch, 1971:** Porto's orchestra still tuned to the hall organ, a little under A440. Rui learned his A there.
- **Equal temperament:** every interval made very slightly wrong, so that every key can be played.
- **Aphasia notes:** many people with aphasia can sing words they can't say.

## Canvas: *Rui's piano*

The thought on slide 5. Writer's notes and connections are in quotes.

> **"Why has he kept / the A flat?"** (note) → **Pitch, 1971** (Page) → **Rui Carvalho** (Entry) → **"Don't correct it. / She tunes the piano to him."** (note) → ***The Flat A*** (Scene · The Second Visit, selected; its preview is the Scene's first paragraph)
