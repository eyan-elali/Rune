# Real keystrokes — provenance

Every `d-*.wav` here is cut from one real recording of a physical keyboard. Nothing is synthesised.

- **Recording:** "Typing.wav" by **Dustin_Davis**, 2017-08-15. Author's note: "I was working on a script and thought about putting my mic over my keyboard… Recorded on a Rode Nt1. No edits." Freesound tags: `loud`, `mechanicalkeyboard`, `type`, `typing`. 12:50.98, WAV, 44.1 kHz, 32-bit float. Both channels are identical (a mono microphone).
- **Origin:** https://freesound.org/people/Dustin_Davis/sounds/399603/
- **Downloaded from:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:399603_dustin-davis_typing.wav (original file). SHA-1 `35b9888564d62e8a67e0ad4fa9e9668c977a33ed`, matching the Commons record.
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (public domain dedication), on both Freesound and Commons. Commercial use is allowed and attribution is not required. Checked 2026-10-15.
- **Extraction:** `node scripts/extract-typing-real.mjs <source.wav>`. The source times of each keystroke are listed in that script.
